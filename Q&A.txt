❓ Q1. Why did you remove the reservation module? Isn’t it required?
✅ STRONG ANSWER

“A Channel Manager should control supply, not demand.
Reservations belong to PMS or OTAs.
To avoid tight coupling and scalability issues, I made the inventory calendar the source of truth.
OTA bookings later just translate into stop-sell events on the calendar.”

💡 IMPRESSION

✔ You understand domain boundaries
✔ You didn’t blindly build CRUD
✔ You know real hotel tech architecture

❓ Q2. How do you calculate revenue without bookings?
✅ ANSWER

“The system calculates potential revenue, not realized revenue.
Revenue is derived from blocked room nights using price overrides or base prices.
This is exactly how channel managers forecast revenue before actual bookings arrive from OTAs.”

💡 IMPRESSION

✔ Business understanding
✔ Terminology clarity (potential vs realized)

❓ Q3. What is your single source of truth in this system?
✅ ANSWER (VERY IMPORTANT)

“The inventory calendar at room level.
Every feature—dashboard, charts, reports—is derived from it.
Nothing writes to the dashboard or reports directly.”

✔ System design clarity

❓ Q4. How does your system handle OTA bookings in future?
✅ ANSWER

“OTA bookings will arrive via webhooks.
Each booking will simply mark corresponding dates as stop-sell in the inventory calendar.
The rest of the system automatically updates through event propagation.”

❓ Q5. How scalable is this system for 1000+ rooms?
✅ ANSWER

“The calendar uses sparse storage—only modified dates are stored.
With backend indexing on (room_id, date), this scales linearly.
The frontend already follows an event-driven model, which maps well to pub/sub systems.”

💡 IMPRESSION

✔ Performance awareness
✔ Backend foresight

❓ Q6. Why did you choose an event-driven approach on frontend?
✅ ANSWER

“It keeps modules loosely coupled.
Inventory emits changes, dashboards and reports subscribe.
This mirrors backend architectures like message queues and WebSockets, making migration easy.”

💡 IMPRESSION

✔ Architectural maturity
✔ Not just UI thinking

❓ Q7. How would you migrate this to backend?
✅ ANSWER (STEP-WISE)

“Step 1: Replace localStorage with REST APIs.
Step 2: Persist inventory and calendar in database tables.
Step 3: Introduce authentication and OTA webhooks.
Frontend logic remains mostly unchanged.”

💡 IMPRESSION

✔ Clear roadmap
✔ Low-risk migration plan

❓ Q8. Why not calculate occupancy from reservations?
✅ ANSWER

“Occupancy is about availability, not bookings.
In a channel manager, blocked inventory represents sold or unavailable rooms.
Using stop-sell ensures consistency even when bookings come from multiple OTAs.”

💡 IMPRESSION

✔ Domain-correct answer
✔ Avoids double counting

❓ Q9. What KPIs does your dashboard support?

❓ Q10. How do you ensure data consistency across modules?
✅ ANSWER

All other modules are read-only derivatives triggered by inventoryUpdated events.”
